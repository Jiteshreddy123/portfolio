# Y. Jitesh Reddy — Personal Portfolio

An architectural, peaceful, and warm portfolio crafted with pastel serenity, humanistic typography, and progressive disclosure UI/UX principles.

---


## 🎨 Design Philosophy & Tone
- **Warm Pastel Palette**: Built around soothing warm linen cream (`#FAF8F5`, `#F3EFE9`), crisp white surfaces, soft borders, and gentle pastel accents (Sage for healthcare & care, Peach for community & optimization, Amber for empirical data, and Slate for systems engineering).
- **Subtle Humanism & Optimism**: Portrays Jitesh's quiet optimism and empathy indirectly through purpose-driven narratives—showing how his software actively eases friction in fragile human moments (e.g. cancer discharge care, harmonious roommate living).
- **The Polymathic Lens**: Clearly structures his cross-disciplinary capabilities into four converging pillars: Systems Architecture, Human-Centered AI, Combinatorial Optimization, and Empirical Intelligence.
- **Architectural UI/UX**: Clear hierarchy without overwhelming visitors. Features progressive disclosure drawers for system blueprints, category filter chips, dynamic local time tracking (IST), and 1-click clipboard actions.
